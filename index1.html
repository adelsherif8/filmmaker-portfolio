01 — About

Cinema is how I
understand the world.

A filmmaking graduate from the British University in Egypt, Omar Emad El Din has spent years developing a voice rooted in authentic human experience — stories drawn from culture, memory, and the quiet tensions of everyday life.

From writing the graduation film Qahwet El Mayteen to directing creative campaigns and recording on documentary sets, Omar works at every layer of the filmmaking process with precision and passion.

02 — Films & Projects

A body of work spanning narrative short film, documentary production, and brand storytelling — each project a study in craft, collaboration, and vision.

001
Qahwet El Mayteen قهوة الميتين
Short Film Graduation Project Scriptwriter

An original screenplay developed for the BUE graduation project — a narrative rooted in Egyptian cultural life, blending dark atmosphere with emotionally resonant character work. From concept through final draft, Omar authored every layer of the story's structure and dialogue.

002
Documentary Film Untitled Documentary Production
Documentary Sound Recordist

On-set audio capture for a documentary production — operating field recording equipment to deliver broadcast-quality sound across varied and demanding locations. Real-time level monitoring, acoustic problem-solving, and seamless coordination with the camera team.

003
Placid Disruption Campaign Placid Essentials — Brand Campaign
Brand Film Commercial Creative Director

Conceived and led the complete creative direction for Placid Essentials' flagship campaign — defining the visual identity, storytelling concept, and tonal language from brief to final cut. Cinematic aesthetics applied to commercial storytelling with a singular, intentional vision.
