01 — About
Director · Cairo · 2024

Cinema is how I
understand the world.

A filmmaking graduate from the British University in Egypt, Omar Emad El Din has spent years developing a voice rooted in authentic human experience — stories drawn from culture, memory, and the quiet tensions of everyday life.

From writing the graduation film Qahwet El Mayteen to directing brand campaigns and recording on documentary sets, Omar works at every layer of the filmmaking process with precision and deep passion for the craft.
