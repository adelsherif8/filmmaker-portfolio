02 — Films & Projects

A body of work spanning narrative short film, documentary production, and brand storytelling — each project a study in craft, collaboration, and singular vision.

001
I
Qahwet El Mayteenقهوة الميتين — The Coffee of the Dead
Short Film Graduation Project Scriptwriter

An original screenplay developed as the British University in Egypt's graduation film — a narrative rooted in Egyptian cultural life and folklore, blending a dark, claustrophobic atmosphere with emotionally resonant character work.

  • Authored the complete original screenplay — dramatic structure, character arcs, and thematic throughlines from concept to final draft.
  • Crafted culturally resonant dialogue rooted in Egyptian social dynamics, folklore, and lived experience.
  • Collaborated with the directing and production team to translate the script into a cohesive visual narrative.
  • Developed the film's tonal language — balancing dark subject matter with moments of human warmth and black humour.
002
II
Documentary FilmUntitled Documentary Production
Documentary On-Set Sound Recordist

On-set audio capture and sound design role on an active documentary production — operating field recording equipment to deliver broadcast-quality audio across varied, demanding locations.

  • Operated boom and field recording equipment, capturing clean, broadcast-quality dialogue and ambient sound under real-world constraints.
  • Monitored levels in real time, adapting to changing on-location acoustics to eliminate noise and maintain sonic continuity.
  • Coordinated with the director and camera team to ensure sound coverage aligned with visual framing choices.
  • Managed cabling, equipment transport, and field prep — maintaining the integrity of the audio chain throughout production.
003
III
Placid DisruptionPlacid Essentials — Brand Campaign
Brand Film Commercial Creative Director

Conceived and led the complete creative direction for Placid Essentials' flagship marketing campaign — defining visual identity, storytelling concept, and tonal language from brief to final deliverable.

  • Conceptualized the campaign's core narrative — "controlled disruption as aesthetic" — translating brand values into cinematic visual language.
  • Directed all visual assets, ensuring consistency of mood, palette, and rhythm across every piece of content.
  • Applied filmmaking storytelling techniques to commercial content, elevating brand identity through cinematic aesthetics.
  • Managed creative execution from initial brief through production, maintaining a singular vision throughout the process.
